VPN AND NETWORK SUPPORT

VPN Access:
Employees working remotely may need to connect to the company VPN to access internal systems and resources.

VPN Connection Problems:
If the VPN does not connect, employees should first verify that their internet connection is working. They should then restart the VPN application and try connecting again.

VPN Credentials:
Employees should use their company credentials when signing in to the VPN. Passwords must never be shared with another person.

VPN Access Request:
If an employee does not have VPN access, they should submit a VPN access request through the IT Service Desk portal. Approval may be required depending on the employee's role.

Internet Connection Problems:
Employees experiencing internet problems should first check whether other websites or applications can connect. They should restart their network connection if necessary.

Internal System Access:
Some internal company systems are accessible only when connected to the company network or VPN. Employees working remotely should connect to the VPN before attempting to access these systems.

Persistent Network Problems:
If basic troubleshooting does not resolve the issue, employees should create an IT Service Desk ticket and include the device name, location, error message, and approximate time when the problem occurred.
