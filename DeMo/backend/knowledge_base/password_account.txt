PASSWORD AND ACCOUNT SUPPORT

Password Reset:
Employees who forget their company password should use the password reset option on the company login page. If self-service password reset is unavailable, the employee should contact the IT Service Desk.

Account Lockout:
An account may become locked after multiple unsuccessful login attempts. Employees should wait for the configured lockout period or contact the IT Service Desk if immediate assistance is required.

Account Unlock:
The IT Service Desk can verify the employee's identity before unlocking a locked account. Employees should never share their password with IT support staff.

Password Security:
Employees must not share their passwords with colleagues, managers, or IT support staff. Passwords should be kept confidential.

Multi-Factor Authentication:
Employees may be required to use multi-factor authentication when accessing company systems. If an employee loses access to their authentication device, they should contact the IT Service Desk for account recovery assistance.

Suspicious Login:
If an employee notices an unfamiliar login or suspects that their password has been compromised, they should immediately contact the IT Service Desk and change their password.
